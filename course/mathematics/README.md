# Mathematics for AI

Learn math alongside code. You do not need to master every proof before beginning, but you do need enough math to understand models, objectives, uncertainty, and failure.

## Chapters

1. [Algebra, functions, and logarithms](01-algebra-functions.md)
2. [Vectors, matrices, and geometry](02-linear-algebra.md)
3. [Probability and statistics](03-probability-statistics.md)
4. [Calculus and gradients](04-calculus-gradients.md)
5. [Optimization and numerical thinking](05-optimization.md)

## Recommended order

Review algebra first. Learn vectors and statistics before classical ML; study derivatives and optimization as you meet gradient descent and neural networks. Return for deeper proofs during advanced study.
