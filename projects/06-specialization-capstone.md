# Project 06 — Choose a specialty and complete a capstone

## Choose one direction

- **Reinforcement learning:** a simulator task with explicit reward, safe constraints, and comparison to a hand-coded policy.
- **Recommendation:** candidate generation and ranking with exposure-aware offline evaluation.
- **Time series:** a forecast horizon, seasonal baseline, chronological backtesting, and error by horizon.
- **Graph learning:** node or edge task, graph-aware split, and comparison to a non-graph baseline.
- **Robotics:** simulation or bounded hardware task with perception, planning/control, emergency stop, and sim-to-real analysis.
- **Research reproduction:** reproduce one narrow claim from a paper, including an honest account of unavailable details or mismatches.

## Capstone contract

Before building, write:
1. the question and intended user;
2. data source, license, collection method, and known limitations;
3. the simplest credible baseline;
4. the evaluation split and metric;
5. success and failure conditions;
6. privacy, safety, and misuse boundaries.

## Milestones

- **Proposal:** a one-page plan reviewed by a peer or mentor.
- **Baseline:** runnable implementation and first result.
- **Controlled experiment:** change one major factor and record the result.
- **Error analysis:** inspect failures and identify a next experiment.
- **Release candidate:** setup guide, versioned data/model/config, evaluation summary, and limitations.
- **Presentation:** explain the method to a beginner and defend the evidence to a technical reader.

## Final evidence

A capstone is complete when someone else can run it, understand the result, identify its limits, and reproduce the evaluation. Score it with the [shared rubric](RUBRIC.md). For advanced work, include an ablation, robustness check, or error-slice analysis; larger model size is not a substitute.
