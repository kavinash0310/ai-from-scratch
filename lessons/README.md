# Course lesson notes

> The files in this folder are companion review notes. The full detailed course is organized by subject under [course/](../course/README.md), including dedicated folders for Python, mathematics, AI, ML, NLP, LLMs, and more.



Welcome. This course is written for a learner starting with no programming or AI background. Go in order the first time. Each lesson has plain-language explanations, detailed notes, examples, questions with answers, and practice.

## How to study a lesson

1. Read one section, then pause and explain the idea in your own words.
2. Type and change any example yourself. Do not only read the solution.
3. Answer the review questions before opening their answers.
4. Complete at least one exercise and write down what confused you.
5. Use the mastery check to decide whether to continue or review.

AI is a wide field. Reaching advanced ability takes continued projects and study after this course; the goal here is to build the foundation and teach you how to keep advancing.

## Practice and mastery system

Pair each lesson with a relevant [runnable lab](../labs/README.md), then complete a [graded project](../projects/README.md). Use [mastery checks](../assessments/MASTERY_CHECKS.md) before moving ahead and record evidence in the [100-point scorecard](../assessments/MASTERY_SCORECARD.md).

## Prerequisites and pace

Start with Lesson 00. You need only basic arithmetic and access to a computer or browser notebook. Lesson 01 teaches programming. Lesson 02 teaches the math gradually; you are not expected to know calculus or linear algebra in advance.

Use the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) for timing and project milestones, [resources](../RESOURCES.md) for outside courses, and [progress tracker](../PROGRESS.md) to record your work.
